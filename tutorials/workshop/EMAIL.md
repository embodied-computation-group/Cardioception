# Email to participants

**Subject:** Cardioception workshop: installation before the session

Dear all,

In the Cardioception workshop we will run the Heart Rate Discrimination task and
analyze a completed session. Please prepare your laptop before we meet by following
these instructions:

https://github.com/embodied-computation-group/Cardioception/blob/master/tutorials/workshop/PREINSTALL.md

The installation usually takes 30 to 45 minutes, mainly for downloads and compilation,
and requires approximately 4 GB of free disk space. You do not need a pulse oximeter.
The software has a test mode for learning the task, and I will demonstrate data
collection with a live pulse oximeter during the workshop.

If you mainly work in R and have not used Python environments before, begin with the
short `GETTING_STARTED.md` guide linked at the top of the installation page.

The final installation step runs a preflight check and asks you to browse the three
notebooks. You do not need to complete any exercises in advance, but a brief look will
make the sequence and terminology familiar. If the preflight check says that
everything is ready, no further action is needed. If a required check fails, please
send me the complete output. There is no need to spend more than 30 minutes trying to
resolve it alone; we can address remaining problems before the session.

Best,

Micah
