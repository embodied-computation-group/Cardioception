# Whitehead method

The two-alternative heartbeat synchrony task of Whitehead and colleagues {cite:p}`1977:whitehead` is coming to Cardioception. Tones are presented at two delays from the ECG R-wave, and participants judge which sequence is simultaneous with their heartbeat.

A review of the literature behind it will follow. Until then, [what fixed-delay synchrony tasks can establish](index.md#what-fixed-delay-synchrony-tasks-can-establish) on the tasks overview describes the method.
