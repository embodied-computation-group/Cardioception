# Whitehead method

An implementation of the two-alternative heartbeat synchrony task of Whitehead and colleagues {cite:p}`1977:whitehead` is planned for Cardioception. In this procedure, tones are presented at two delays from the ECG R-wave, and participants judge which sequence is simultaneous with their heartbeat.

A review of the literature will accompany the implementation. For an introduction to the method and its limitations, see [What fixed-delay synchrony tasks can establish](theory.md#what-fixed-delay-synchrony-tasks-can-establish).
